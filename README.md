# 3:17 — Film Production Package

A premium Egyptian crime mystery thriller film concept in the style of a realistic Netflix crime drama, grounded in modern Egypt and designed around a slow-burn investigation, psychological tension, and a devastating final twist.

## Overview

Title: 3:17
Genre: Crime Mystery Thriller
Setting: Modern-day Egypt
Runtime: 110–125 minutes
Aspect Ratio: 16:9
Visual Style: Ultra-realistic, cinematic, grounded in realism
Tone: Dark, tense, intelligent, atmospheric, psychologically suspenseful

## Story Premise

At exactly 3:17 AM, a mysterious emergency call is placed from Adam Mansour’s phone. The call contains only heavy breathing, a metallic impact, and a whisper: “Don’t look behind you.” When police break into Adam’s apartment, they find his body on the floor, the front door locked from inside, the television still running, and a wall clock frozen at 3:17.

On the desk lies a single handwritten note: “I KNOW WHO DID IT.”

Detective Samer begins investigating a case that becomes more and more impossible: the apartment shows no forced entry, a friend is suddenly the prime suspect, secrets begin unraveling inside Adam’s life, and a hidden digital message reveals that Adam may not have died at the time the police believe.
