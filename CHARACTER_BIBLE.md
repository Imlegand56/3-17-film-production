# Character Bible — 3:17

## DETECTIVE SAMER

### Character Overview
Detective Samer is the central investigator of the case. He is a serious Egyptian detective in his early 40s, careful, observant, and emotionally controlled. He rarely raises his voice. He takes notes. He studies the room as if it is telling him a story. He is not driven by ego or aggression. His strength comes from discipline, logic, and the ability to detect contradictions.

### Age
42

### Appearance
- Egyptian male
- short dark hair with subtle gray
- trimmed beard
- dark charcoal detective suit
- black shirt
- formal black shoes
- serious, intelligent eyes
- calm and controlled expression
- exact appearance must remain consistent at all times

### Personality
- calm, deliberate, observant
- methodical and patient
- skeptical but fair
- emotionally reserved
- highly intelligent
- quietly intense
- not easily fooled

### Narrative Function
Samer is the lens through which the audience learns the truth. He is not a flashy detective; he is a realist. The mystery deepens around him, and each new clue forces him to question his assumptions.

### Continuity Notes
- Must maintain a consistent dark suit, black shirt, exact hair and beard styling in all scenes.
- His physical features must remain unchanged in both present-day scenes and flashback context.
- He should remain visually stable across all investigations, interviews, and final reveal sequence.

## ADAM MANSOUR

### Character Overview
Adam is a seemingly ordinary but intelligent Egyptian man in his mid-30s. He lives alone in an old apartment and appears to be a quiet, modest individual, but he is more observant and strategic than he seems. He is patient, calculating, and determined to uncover the truth before anyone else is hurt.

### Age
35

### Appearance
- Egyptian male
- medium build
- short black hair
- light beard
- ordinary but intelligent appearance
- casual but neat clothing
- exact appearance must remain consistent in all flashbacks and recordings

### Personality
- observant
- quiet but sharp
- strategic
- emotionally controlled
- capable of deception when necessary
- protective of his own truth

### Narrative Function
Adam is the central mystery. He is the victim, the witness, the architect of the trap, and the hidden source of the evidence. His video confessions reveal that he was not a passive victim but someone who intentionally staged an apparent death to expose a criminal system.

### Continuity Notes
- Must remain consistent in every flashback, surveillance sequence, and recorded video.
- His clothing in videos and recordings should match his established everyday look.
- His age and physical features must remain consistent across all scenes.

## RAMY

### Character Overview
Ramy is Adam’s close friend and the first major suspect in the case. He is a nervous, emotional man whose concern for Adam seems genuine, but his actions are suspicious enough to make him a natural target for investigators. He is not a mastermind; he is a frightened witness who becomes entangled in a much larger truth.

### Age
36

### Appearance
- Egyptian male
- medium build
- short black hair
- clean-shaven
- nervous eyes
- dark casual clothing
- exact appearance must remain consistent throughout the film

### Personality
- anxious
- loyal
- emotionally reactive
- defensive under pressure
- not highly strategic, but motivated by fear
- genuine concern for Adam

### Narrative Function
Ramy is the first suspect and the emotional bridge between Adam’s private world and the larger criminal conspiracy. His tension creates suspicion and escalates the investigation. He is later revealed as a man who was searching for evidence, not a killer.

### Continuity Notes
- Dark casual clothing from first appearance to final confrontation must remain visually consistent.
- His nervous behavior should feel natural and not exaggerated.
- His reactions must remain coherent as the plot shifts from suspicion to clarification.

## MAZEN

### Character Overview
Mazen is Adam’s older brother, a mysterious and darker figure who appears to embody the hidden danger at the center of the story. He is older than Adam, more intense in expression, and carries an air of secrecy. His return to the city raises suspicion and helps explain why Adam was frightened.

### Age
40

### Appearance
- Egyptian male
- darker complexion
- short black hair
- trimmed beard
- mysterious expression
- reserved and intense
- exact appearance must remain consistent whenever shown

### Personality
- guarded
- emotionally distant
- unpredictable
- possibly dangerous
- connected to weaponized fear and hidden danger

### Narrative Function
Mazen’s return intensifies the fear around Adam. His absence becomes a central structural clue. While not fully central at first, his presence is tied to the investigation’s emotional and practical threat.

### Continuity Notes
- Must remain visually stable whenever he appears in memory, police investigation, or surveillance-based context.
- His facial features and expression should never shift stylization across scenes.

## SUPPORTING POLICE/FORENSIC PRESENCE

### Narrative Function
The supporting investigators provide realism, procedural grounding, and timeline confirmation. Their role is to support the film’s credibility without overshadowing Samer’s quiet authority.

### Visual and Story Requirements
- Realistic police procedures
- Strong practical detail in evidence handling
- Distant radio chatter, subtle ambient city noise, low-level police operation atmosphere
- No over-the-top action or exaggerated cinematic procedural flourishes

## CONTINUITY RULES

1. All characters must maintain exact visual consistency in every scene.
2. Adam’s apartment must remain visually stable across all investigation scenes.
3. The wall clock must be consistent in appearance and position.
4. The desk, the note, and the hidden memory device must remain physically coherent.
5. The timeline must hold: the emergency call occurs at 3:17; the police arrive at 3:31; the black car appears at 3:05; Adam leaves the apartment and returns to the trap sequence in a later reveal.
6. The final twist must feel inevitable but not obvious from the start.

## Character Arc Summary

- Samer moves from disbelief to structured revelation.
- Adam moves from potential victim to strategic manipulator of truth.
- Ramy moves from suspect to frightened witness to ally in discovery.
- Mazen represents the hidden threat controlling the deeper tension.

The story turns on the idea that the truth is not always hidden in a murder—it is hidden in the way the victim chooses to leave the evidence behind.
